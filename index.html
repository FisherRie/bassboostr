Common questions

Why can't I import some Apple Music songs?
Apple Music subscription downloads are copy-protected, so iOS doesn't let other apps read them. Songs you've bought in iTunes or synced from a computer work, and so do audio files and videos.

My iPhone doesn't vibrate.
Turn on Bass Boost, check that Haptic Vibration isn't off in BASSBOOSTR Settings, and make sure System Haptics is on in iOS Settings > Sounds & Haptics.

How do I restore BASSBOOSTR Lifetime on a new iPhone?
Open Settings in BASSBOOSTR and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

What does the free version include?
Everything except Bass Boost and the last 20% of the bass dial. Once a day you can watch an ad to unlock both for one song. BASSBOOSTR Lifetime unlocks them for good and removes ads.
