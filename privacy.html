BASSBOOSTR Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

BASSBOOSTR is a music player that boosts the bass of songs and videos you import and vibrates your iPhone with the beat. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your music stays on your iPhone

Songs, videos, screen recordings and covers you import are processed and stored only on your device. BASSBOOSTR has no accounts and no servers of its own, and never uploads your media. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored.

Music library access

If you choose to import from your Music library, iOS asks for permission first. BASSBOOSTR only reads the songs you pick so it can copy their audio into the app. You can turn this off any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Media & Apple Music.

Purchases

BASSBOOSTR Lifetime is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether the purchase is active on your Apple Account.

Advertising

If you haven't bought BASSBOOSTR Lifetime, the app shows ads from Google AdMob between songs and when you choose to watch an ad to unlock a song. To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers (such as the identifier for vendor) and approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with;
  • crash and performance data from the ads SDK.

BASSBOOSTR does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so it does not give Google access to your device's advertising identifier. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Buying BASSBOOSTR Lifetime removes all ads, and the ads SDK stops requesting ads.

Settings stored on your device

Your preferences (bass level, vibration strength, queue, app icon) are saved on your iPhone so the app remembers them. They are not sent anywhere.

Children

BASSBOOSTR is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
